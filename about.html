About MonsterCodex

The Ultimate Monster Encyclopedia

MonsterCodex is a growing database dedicated to monsters, creatures and mysterious beings from movies, games, television, folklore, mythology, Creepypasta and beyond.

👹 What is MonsterCodex?

MonsterCodex is designed to bring monsters from different universes together in one searchable database.

Each monster has its own profile containing information about its origins, appearances, abilities, weaknesses and other notable characteristics.

🔮 A Growing Database

MonsterCodex is an ongoing project and the database will continue to grow with new monsters, characters and universes being added over time.

The goal is simple: create a place where monster fans can explore, discover and learn about creatures from every corner of horror, fantasy, science fiction and mythology.
